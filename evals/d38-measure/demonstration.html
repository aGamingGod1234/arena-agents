What the evidence can establish

All 15 fatal traces include retained protocol and live server reflex status, plus dispatches, receipts, inputs and damage evidence. Expired or disabled rules do not count as live coverage. Watcher rule names alone do not establish it.

Definitive hands/aim owners, complete attributed damage dealt, and causal earned/spent resource quantities remain unknown where the logs do not prove them. Inventory gains can include supplies or recovery. Death loss is inferred from the last living sample; it does not prove destruction.

Builds, seeds, supplied kits and budgets differ. These are observational stage scores, not a controlled fresh-game success rate. The 23 sampled outputs have 21 budget endings, one infrastructure failure and one unknown early ending.
